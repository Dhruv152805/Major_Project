Bidding Formula Comparison

Evaluation of previous formula vs. active enhanced formula with safety multiplier m

Safety Rule: m = 0 if Battery < 10%, else 1
PREVIOUS SPECIFICATION ⚠️ No low-battery protection
Bidold = (wd · D) + (wb · (1 - B)) + (wf · F) + Penalties
Produces positive bids regardless of critical battery, which can cause a depleted robot to win and stall mid-mission.
ENHANCED SPECIFICATION (ACTIVE) 🛡️ Protected with variable m
Bidenhanced = m × [ (wd · D) + (wb · (1 - B)) + (wf · F) + Penalties ]
When battery drops below 10%, m = 0 automatically zeroes the bid (disqualification) to award the task to a healthy peer.
